# Face Attendance App — Teacher and Student User Guide

This guide explains how teachers and students should use face registration, recognition,
verification, and attendance screens.

---

## 1. Before Using Face Scan

- Clean the front camera.
- Stand in normal indoor lighting.
- Remove anything covering the eyes or most of the face.
- Hold the phone at face level.
- Keep only one face inside the oval.
- Use your live face. Do not use a printed photo or a photo displayed on another device.
- Follow the spoken instruction and the message shown on the screen.

### Camera guide colours

- **Yellow:** Face is missing or needs adjustment.
- **Blue:** Face is detected and being checked.
- **Green:** Face and liveness checks are ready.
- **Red:** Face was not accepted or did not match.

Five green dots may appear on the eyes, nose, and mouth. Keep these points visible inside the
camera area.

---

## 2. Face Registration

Face registration is normally completed with help from an authorised operator.

1. Select whether the person is a **Student** or **Teacher**.
2. Select the correct person.
3. Confirm that the displayed name and ID are correct.
4. Start face registration.
5. Place the face inside the oval and look straight at the camera.
6. Keep both eyes open until the app asks for a blink.
7. Blink slowly once, then open both eyes.
8. Hold still while the app captures three clear face samples.
9. Wait for the successful registration message.

### If the face is already registered

A popup displays the registered person's:

- Name
- ID
- Role
- Face match percentage

Do not register the face for another person. If the popup shows the same person and the
operator is updating the record, use the available update option.

---

## 3. Teacher — Start an Attendance Session

1. Open the attendance section.
2. Select or scan the correct classroom.
3. Open the teacher face-scanning screen.
4. Place your face inside the oval.
5. Look straight at the camera with both eyes open.
6. Blink slowly once when instructed.
7. Keep still until your face is verified.
8. Check that your name is shown correctly.
9. Confirm that student attendance should start.
10. Wait for the spoken message: **“Attendance session started. Students may now scan their
    faces.”**

The student attendance screen will then open.

### Teacher face not matched

- Make sure you selected the correct classroom.
- Improve the lighting.
- Move closer and keep the whole face inside the oval.
- Blink once only when instructed.
- Try again without moving the phone.
- If the app says you are not assigned to a class, contact the administrator.

---

## 4. Student — Mark Attendance

1. Wait until the teacher starts the attendance session.
2. Stand in front of the phone one person at a time.
3. Place your face inside the oval.
4. Look straight at the camera with both eyes open.
5. Blink slowly once when instructed.
6. Open both eyes and hold still.
7. Wait for the spoken confirmation.

Successful attendance says:

> “Thank you, [student name]. Your attendance is marked.”

Also check that your name appears as the latest student and that the present count updates.

### Other student messages

- **Attendance already marked:** No further action is required.
- **Already present in another active class:** Contact the teacher.
- **Face not matched:** Adjust your position, blink once when instructed, and retry.
- **Not registered or not enrolled:** Contact the administrator.
- **No active session:** Ask the teacher to start the attendance session.

Do not leave the camera until the app confirms the attendance result.

---

## 5. Teacher — Finish or Manage a Session

While the student attendance screen is open, the active session teacher can scan their face
again to manage the session.

The camera stops while a session popup is open. It cannot recognize another face behind the
popup.

### If no students were scanned

The **Empty Session** popup provides:

- **Mass Bunk:** Record the session as a mass absence and continue to the next required step.
- **Start Cycle:** Close the popup and return to student face scanning. The camera restarts.
- **Discard Cycle:** Delete the empty session. A second confirmation is required.

If **Cancel** is selected in the discard confirmation, the app returns to the student screen
and restarts the camera.

### If one or more students were scanned

The **Session Completed** popup provides:

- **Proceed:** Continue to close/save the session and select the required periods.
- **Mistakenly Started:** Request deletion of the session and its marked attendance. A second
  confirmation is required.

If a confirmation is cancelled and the app returns to the student screen, face scanning
restarts automatically.

---

## 6. Face Recognition Screen

Use this screen to identify a registered student or teacher without marking attendance.

1. Select **Students** or **Teachers**.
2. For students, select a class or **All Classes**.
3. Wait until the app says it is ready.
4. Place one face inside the oval.
5. Look straight at the camera.
6. Blink slowly once when instructed.
7. Hold still until the result popup appears.
8. Confirm the displayed name and role.
9. Select **Continue** to scan another person.

If no registered faces exist for the selection, choose another user type/class or complete
registration first.

---

## 7. Direct Face Verification

Some actions may open a face-verification screen for a specific student.

1. Confirm the displayed student name.
2. Place the student's face inside the oval.
3. Blink slowly once when instructed.
4. Hold still until verification completes.

The app allows up to three attempts. After a successful match, it speaks:

> “Thank you, [student name]. Your face is verified.”

If all attempts fail, contact the administrator instead of repeatedly retrying.

---

## 8. Quick Problem Guide

| Message or problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Face not detected | Move the full face inside the oval and look at the camera. |
| Improve lighting | Face a light source; avoid a bright window behind you. |
| Move closer | Bring the phone closer while keeping the whole face visible. |
| Image is blurry | Hold the phone and face still; clean the camera lens. |
| Look straight | Keep the face level and directly face the camera. |
| Blink instruction remains | Open both eyes, blink slowly once, and reopen them. |
| Face not matched | Check lighting and position, then complete the blink again. |
| No registered faces found | Select the correct role/class or complete registration. |
| Wrong name is displayed | Do not continue; inform the teacher or administrator. |
| Camera permission required | Allow camera permission in the device's app settings. |
| Camera stops during a popup | This is expected. It restarts when returning to scanning. |

---

## 9. Correct Use Checklist

Before accepting a scan result, confirm:

- The correct classroom/session is selected.
- The displayed name belongs to the person in front of the camera.
- The person used a live face and completed the blink.
- A student received the attendance-marked confirmation.
- The teacher completed or saved the session before leaving.
